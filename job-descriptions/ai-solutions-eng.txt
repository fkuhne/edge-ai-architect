Description -

We are seeking an innovative and results-driven AI Solution Engineer to design, develop, and deploy next-generation AI-powered solutions that transform document workflows, business processes, and customer experiences. This role will leverage technologies such as Artificial Intelligence (AI), Large Language Models (LLMs), Natural Language Processing (NLP), AI Agents, Computer Vision, and Edge AI to create intelligent solutions for enterprise customers.

As a key member of the imaging team, you will work closely with product management, imaging and software architects, and development partners to prototype, develop, integrate, and deploy imaging algorithms and scalable AI solutions that deliver measurable business value. You will help identify emerging technology opportunities, translate customer and business requirements into innovative products, and bring new imaging and AI-enabled capabilities to market.

The ideal candidate combines strong software engineering skills with hands-on experience in AI application development, image science, machine learning technologies, and cloud-based services. You are passionate about innovation, thrive in a collaborative environment, and enjoy solving complex business challenges through technology.

Responsibilities

Design, develop, and deploy AI-powered applications and solutions that improve productivity, automate workflows, and enhance customer experiences.
Prototype and evaluate emerging technologies, including Large Language Models (LLMs), Natural Language Processing (NLP), AI Agents, Computer Vision, and Edge AI solutions.
Develop intelligent document workflow solutions that leverage AI to extract insights, automate decisions, and streamline business processes.

Work with internal and external development partners to deliver projects on schedule and meet quality objectives.
Stay current with industry trends and emerging technologies, identifying opportunities to incorporate innovative AI capabilities into future products and solutions.
Mentor and provide technical guidance to junior engineers and other team members.
